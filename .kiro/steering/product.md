# Product Overview

Glocure is a Flutter-based e-commerce mobile application for skincare and beauty products. The app integrates with Shopify's Storefront and Admin GraphQL APIs to provide a comprehensive shopping experience.

## Core Features

- Product browsing and search
- Category-based navigation
- Product filtering and sorting
- Discounted products showcase
- Brand logos and promotional banners
- Skin Genius analysis feature
- Shopping cart and wishlist
- User authentication and language selection

## Business Domain

The app focuses on skincare and beauty products with features like:
- Top products and trending items
- Browse by categories
- Skin analysis recommendations
- Brand showcases
- Promotional banners (top, middle, bottom)
